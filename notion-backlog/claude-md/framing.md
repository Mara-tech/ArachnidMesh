## Framing

**What is authoritative, in this order:** framing documents and specifications (`NEED`, `PREF`, `ARCH`, specification rules), then automated tests, then the code. When two disagree, the higher one wins — a disagreement is a defect to fix or a question to ask. **A need never describes a solution**, and the ranking, the preferences and the decisions belong to the project owner. See [.claude/rules/framing.md](.claude/rules/framing.md).
